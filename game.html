Bus Bounce 2D

Use the ACCELERATE button to move the bus upwards. Be careful not to run into any traffic cones! Use the PLAY AGAIN button to reset the game.


Do you have what it takes to be the best School Bus Driver ever? You're running late for school, hurry up and find out!


This javascript game comes courtesy of W3 Schools. I made modifications to the background image, the character, the score color, the obstacles' color, and the play buttons.
