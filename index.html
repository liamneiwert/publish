About Me

My name is Liam Neiwert and I study Medium and Heavy Duty Truck Technology at Saint Paul College in Saint Paul, Minnesota. My planned graduation date is May 2027 with a diploma in Medium and Heavy Duty Truck Technology. I am taking a web development class over the summer to broaden my skillset. I made this website as a portfolio of my developing skills regarding websites.

In addition to taking classes, I'm also a full time fleet mechanic at American Student Transportation in Blaine, MN working on everything from skid steers to minivans to full-size pick-up trucks to Type-A short buses and Type-C school buses and plow trucks. I started there in December 2025, and was awarded the "Get it Done" Award at the end of the school year for my ability to get it done.

This image features me standing in front of an IC CE bus shortly after passing my road test to obtain my CDL.

Proficient in:

If you have anything with wheels OR an engine that isn't working right, bring it my way and I'll get you back on track.
